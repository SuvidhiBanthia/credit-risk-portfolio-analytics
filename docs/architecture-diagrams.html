3 Model Pipeline & Governance

Customer-grouped split, tuned on train only, evaluated once on the held-out set; out-of-fold scores feed the dashboard.

Compliance & monitoring
Logistic regression and XGBoost are statistically tied on this data (AUC 0.689 vs 0.684). XGBoost is the scoring model for native SHAP and missing-value handling; logistic regression is the explainable benchmark.

Needed from the bank to ship: (1) the default definition and dated outcomes; (2) point-in-time bureau pulls at application; (3) dated monthly repayments and balances; (4) recovery / LGD data; (5) SSO, role-based access and a case-management API; (6) a compliance ruling on permitted attributes (age) and adverse-action reason mapping.
